About Nathalie

Candidate for Mayor of Hamilton · 2026

My Background

Why I am running

I am running because I love Hamilton and believe the City needs stronger accountability, financial discipline and a renewed focus on residents.

My main priority is making Hamilton a better-run city: financially responsible, transparent, safe, and focused on the services residents actually need.

My approach

My experience has taught me to question documents, examine evidence, listen to people and persist when a problem is difficult.

I believe municipal government should focus on the people of Hamilton rather than party politics.

Residents deserve clear information about decisions that affect their homes, businesses, communities and taxes.

What I bring

Accountability

A commitment to asking questions, examining information and expecting clear answers.

Problem-Solving

A practical approach to difficult issues and a willingness to examine evidence before making decisions.

Resiliency

Persistence and determination when problems are complex or difficult to resolve.

Open Minded

Listening to different perspectives and considering information before reaching conclusions.

Attention to Detail

Careful review of documents, policies and practical consequences.

Professional Negotiation

Experience involving communication, negotiation and working toward practical solutions.
