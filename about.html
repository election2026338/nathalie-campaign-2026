About Nathalie

Her background, education, public service and the experience that brought her to public life in Hamilton.

Who I Am

I am Nathalie Xian Yi Yan, and I am running for Mayor of Hamilton in the 2026 municipal election.

My background has taken me through science, medicine, professional regulation, education, governance, legislation and public affairs.

Those experiences have shaped the way I approach problems: look carefully at the information, ask questions, listen to different perspectives and keep working toward a practical solution.

Why Hamilton

Hamilton is the community where I have built my life and where I believe public service can make a meaningful difference.

I have spent many years following public affairs, attending meetings and hearings, speaking with residents and paying attention to the decisions that shape our city.

That experience is part of why I decided to seek the responsibility of serving as Mayor.

My Educational Beginning

My education and professional journey began in pharmaceutical biochemistry. This original graduation document is part of that history.

Why I Am Running

I am running because I love Hamilton and believe the City needs stronger accountability, financial discipline and a renewed focus on residents.

My goal is to help make Hamilton a better-run city: financially responsible, transparent, safe and focused on the services residents actually need.

My Approach

I believe municipal government should focus on practical decisions rather than political slogans.

Residents deserve clear information about decisions that affect their homes, businesses, communities and taxes.

Listen carefully. Examine the evidence. Ask difficult questions. Then act responsibly.
